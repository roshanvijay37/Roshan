Saver – Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026. Saver (com.roshanvijay.saver) is made by Sumathi Enterprises / Roshan Vijay.

No account, no tracking

Saver has no sign-up and no login. It contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking SDKs, and it does not read your contacts, photos, location or device identifiers.

The link you paste

When you paste or share a link into Saver, the link is sent to our server (ig.roshanvijay.com) so it can fetch that public post's video or audio and send the file back to your phone. The server keeps a technical log (time, the link, the requesting IP address and whether it worked) to keep the service running and stop abuse; it is not used to identify you and is never shared or sold.

Files you save

Downloaded videos and audio are saved on your phone only (Movies/Saver and Music/Saver). We never see or upload your files. You can delete them from your gallery or file manager at any time.

Permissions

  • Internet – to look up links and download the files.
  • Storage (Android 8 and 9 only) – to save the file in your Movies or Music folder. Android 10 and later need no permission for this.

Deleting data

There is no account or stored personal data to delete. Uninstalling the app removes it completely; files you saved stay until you delete them.
