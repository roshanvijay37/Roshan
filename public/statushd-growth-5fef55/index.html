Three conclusions follow from this table.

  1. Your bottleneck is not awareness. It is one step ("send it as a Document") and one ceiling (one laptop). Ads poured in now mostly buy people who get stuck.
  2. Capacity caps revenue. At ~15 videos an hour, about 20 hours a day, you deliver roughly 250–300 videos a day. Check the Status editor on the S24 and the Redmi every week. Plan every rupee to pay back within 30 days, because this business could get a 3-month warning or none.

What you're missing

  1. Play search is the market's main acquisition channel, and you're effectively absent from it. Your title is "StatusHD", with no keywords.
  2. The app is the fix for the Document step. On Android the app attaches the file as a Document automatically. Android users should go to the app; iPhone users should go to WhatsApp. Today everyone is sent to WhatsApp and asked to find a menu they've never used.
  3. Nobody owns the YouTube intent. Hundreds of thousands of views a month go to tutorials full of "not working" comments. Those viewers are pre-qualified, frustrated, and searching right now.
  4. Timing. Dasara and Navratri are about now (Mangaluru Dasara with Pili Vesha tiger dancers, and your best proof clip is a tiger). Diwali is around 8 November, then wedding season November–February. These are the highest-intent weeks of the year for this product, and they start in days.

What decides whether an ad lives or dies, in this order

  1. Cost per first video: target ≤ ₹60. Kill above ₹150 after ₹600 spent.
  2. Chat → first video ≥ 40%.
  3. Cost per chat ≤ ₹25.
  4. First → second video ≥ 50%.
  5. Cost per paying customer ≤ ₹250 (one month's revenue). If that isn't met by day 21, the channel doesn't scale.

Don't judge anything on CPM, CTR or likes. Scale rule: under ₹60 per first video for three days in a row → raise the budget 30% every two days. Stop raising when cost per first video climbs 50% or the laptop's queue regularly backs up.

Creative rules

  • 9:16, 10–15 seconds.
  • The zoomed before/after in the first 2 seconds.
  • On-screen text in Hinglish or Kannada; captions on, sound optional.
  • End frame: "Tap → send your video as a Document → HD back in 1 minute. First 3 free."
  • The pre-filled message's code must be worth keeping: "Hi! I want my 3 free HD videos (FB6)". People deleted plain codes (FB4); they won't delete their free gift.

Budget

  • Week 1: ₹300–500/day, split across 3 creatives.
  • Week 2: move the money to the winner.
  • Diwali week: scale the winner to ₹1,000–1,500/day only if the cost per first video holds and the queue keeps up.

Landing page

For Meta, the landing page is the WhatsApp chat itself: the auto-reply. For the website, the message is one line plus proof plus the next step:

"Phone mehenga, Status dhundhla?" → before/after slider → "Android: get the app (it does the Document step for you). iPhone: tap here."

Benchmarks to plan against (India, 2025–26, agency estimates)

  • Click-to-WhatsApp: no published benchmark; plan on ₹10–40 per conversation started.
  • WhatsApp Status ads (live in India since 2025, global Feb 2026): vendor estimates CPM ₹25–90, ₹12–60 per chat. Your own test is the best data that exists.
  • Messages inside the 72-hour window an ad opens are free.

06Organic growth loops

1. "How is your Status so clear?" (the core loop)

Your product's result is seen by every contact of every user, but it carries no branding. The trigger is the moment a friend asks.

  • Manual version now: after a person's second successful video, send: "If anyone asks how your Status is so clear, forward them this 👇" followed by a forwardable card with their link statushd.in/go?c=R-<name>.
  • Both people get 3 free videos when the friend sends their first one. Credit only on that first video, the way CRED rewards only after a real action.
  • Your code tracking already supports this.

2. Photographer loop (highest leverage per hour)

A wedding videographer sends the teaser through StatusHD and gives the couple the HD version to post. 2 people post it to about 400 contacts, and guests ask whose work it is. The videographer looks better and you get introduced. Offer: free for 2 months, then ₹499 a month for unlimited client deliveries. One videographer is 20–40 deliveries a month to pre-qualified people. Ten videographers in Mangaluru, Udupi and Bengaluru beat ₹20k of ads.

3. Credit in the caption (test it before deciding; it needs a product change later)

"Sent from my iPhone" built Hotmail. First test on a real phone whether a caption on the returned video survives forwarding to Status.

  • If it does: free-tier videos carry a small "HD via statushd.in" caption the user can delete; paid videos never do.
  • The opt-out matters. A forced watermark is PureStatus's most-hated feature.
  • If the caption doesn't survive forwarding, drop the idea.

4. Turn customer results into ads

Ask every happy user: "Can we use this as a before/after?" Ten real customer clips (a child's dance, a temple festival, a bike ride) beat everything you can make yourself, and keep the creative fresh.

5. Ride the festivals

Dasara (now), Diwali (~8 Nov), weddings (Nov–Feb), Christmas and New Year, Sankranti. Each needs one creative made a week ahead. Night scenes and fireworks are where WhatsApp compression looks worst, which makes them your most dramatic proof.

07Influencers: yes, but not the ones you'd think

  1. YouTube tutorial owners who already rank: the best value.
    • Targets: Partner Tube (9 lakh views), Info Tube (7.9L), Upcoming World (7.7L), ADOPIX (6.3L), Hindi Android Tips, Technical Wiz (iPhone), Mr India Tech, Fabing Tech, Sagar HL Gowdru 7 (Kannada).
    • Their old videos already get traffic, and their comments are full of "not working". Ask for:
      • (a) a pinned comment plus a description link on their top video: offer ₹1,000–2,500 one-time;
      • (b) a new dedicated "2026 working method" video showing StatusHD with a proof zoom: ₹4,000–10,000 for small and mid channels; regional channels are 40–60% cheaper.
    • Approach: email or Instagram DM, in Hindi or Kannada, with the before/after clip attached. "Your video's comments say the trick stopped working. This fixes it; I'll pay to pin a comment that helps your viewers."
  2. Nano creators (2k–20k followers), regional. Kannada/Tulu tech-tips pages, Mangaluru reel makers, wedding filmmakers. Offer free unlimited for 3 months plus ₹1,000–3,000 for one Reel (with permission to run it as an ad). Expect only 10–30% of free-product offers to turn into posts; pay the ones who deliver.
  3. Not micro or macro creators yet. ₹8k–3.5 lakh per Reel, a broad audience, and you can't absorb the traffic.

Tracking

  • A unique link per creator, statushd.in/go?c=YT-PARTNERTUBE.
  • The pre-filled message "Hi! I want my 3 free HD videos (YT-PARTNERTUBE)". The free gift is the reason people keep the code.
  • Results read through ad_report.py, which already joins codes to first videos and subscriptions.
  • Pay the next video only to creators whose code produced at least one paying customer per ₹1,000 spent.

09Brutal criticism

What's weak about the current marketing

  • You've sent paid traffic into a 5-step flow with an unfamiliar step, then judged the ad. ₹289 → 1 video is a funnel problem, not a creative problem.
  • The time is going to the engineering, not the customers. 867 tests, dozens of pipeline fixes, Reddit and YouTube scripting automation — and from what's on record, no conversation with the 21 people who sent one video and left. Talking to 15 users this week is worth more than any channel.
  • Three channels with nothing to show: Reddit (shadowbanned), YouTube comment replies (all held), and the creators campaign (zero delivery).
  • Instagram @status_hd_37 has 0 posts. A curious ad viewer who checks you sees an empty account.
  • The Play app is your Android funnel fix, and nobody is sent to it.
  • The brand is generic and already copied. Three other apps called StatusHD. File a trademark: about ₹4,500 per class for an individual or startup.

What you're likely misunderstanding about the customer

  • They don't want HD. They want to not look bad, usually around a specific event. You sell "unlimited for 30 days"; they need it for a wedding, a festival, a new phone.
  • They don't trust "HD Status fixes". They've been burned by "1000% working" videos. Your proof must be stronger than everyone else's, every time.
  • The "privacy" paragraph ("the person who runs it can open any file") is honest and right to keep, but it's a conversion killer for wedding and family videos. Add the reassurance that is true: deleted after delivery, never shared, a real named person in Mangaluru.

Why people might not pay

  • Free alternatives exist, or seem to.
  • ₹250 a month for something used 2–4 times a month feels expensive. Competitors' weekly passes at ₹99 exist because of this.
  • Paying a stranger's personal UPI ID and sending a screenshot looks like a scam.
  • The trial ends before anyone has reacted to their post.

What could kill the business

  1. WhatsApp ships HD Status. Overnight death; nothing you can do but pay back fast.
  2. The number gets banned. Automating WhatsApp Web breaks WhatsApp's terms, and ads that send hundreds of strangers to that number create exactly the pattern their systems look for. Keep the spare number warm. Never scale ad volume faster than about 2× a week.
  3. The laptop. One machine and one Chrome that crashes 5–11 times an hour. A second machine (a ₹25–35k mini PC) plus a second number is a better ₹30k than any ad spend once a channel works.
  4. Copycats with the same model (Vector Inc) and a cash-rich incumbent (PureStatus) holding the Play search results.

Assumptions to check this month

  1. Do strangers pay? (Not friends; count them separately.)
  2. What is the real cost per paying customer on one channel?
  3. Packs or subscription?
  4. Does a longer trial (3 videos, no time limit) raise payment?
  5. Do people forward correctly, or re-post from the gallery?
  6. Does a caption survive forwarding to Status?
  7. Has WhatsApp shipped an HD Status toggle to anyone yet?

Change immediately (business settings, not code):

  • Trial = 3 free videos, no time limit, instead of 1 day. Your access config already has free_videos: 3, so this is a settings decision for you to make.
  • Payment through a payment page in the business name (a Razorpay or Cashfree payment link or UPI business QR) instead of a personal ID plus screenshot.
  • Test packs alongside the monthly plan: ₹49 for 10 HD videos (valid 30 days) versus ₹250 a month. Packs match how people actually use it, and capacity.
  • Personal replies to stuck leads within 5 minutes, 9am–11pm.

Stop completely: Reddit; @statushdin comment replies; interest-only creator targeting; new pipeline features without a customer asking for them; hiding the price on the site. Hiding it raises chat count but wastes capacity on people who'd never pay ₹250, and "first 3 free" is the better hook anyway. Test showing it.

Pricing benchmarks (India)

  • PureStatus: ₹99/week compress pass, ₹299/month; premium ₹199/week, ₹2,999/year.
  • Clear Status: ₹69–99/week, ₹175–249/month.
  • Smaller apps: ₹79–250. Vector Inc: 2 free a day with a watermark.
  • India prefers small "sachet" plans; people distrust auto-debit trials.

12The one strategy I'd bet on

Buy the frustration that already exists on YouTube, and convert it with proof.

There are hundreds of thousands of views a month on tutorials whose comment sections say "not working", "fake", "phir bhi blur" ("still blurry"). These people are searching right now, already know the problem, and have already failed with the free fix. Nobody owns this traffic.

Buying a pinned comment and a description link on a video with 9 lakh views is a one-time cost that keeps working, it doesn't spike traffic in a way that gets your number banned, and it fits one laptop. Meta then becomes the controllable amplifier for the festival weeks.
