The captions

Written the way a real owner types — dry, understated, no "POV / bestie / *asterisk gag" formula. The whole clip is one relatable thought every dog owner has had: the bored dog stuck inside when it rains, staring at you until you give in.

Reordered to open on the stare. That expressive look down the lens happens around nine seconds into the take — it's the scroll-stopper, so it's frame one. The rest tells the little story: rain → boredom → "the face" → giving in → worth it.

One shot has no caption on purpose. Real clips let the dog breathe; captioning every second is what makes a short feel over-produced.
