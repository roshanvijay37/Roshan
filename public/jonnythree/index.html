The joke

Jonny meets a cow on his morning walk and decides, with total confidence, that he has made a new best friend. The cow does not agree. The captions are Jonny's inner monologue — earnest, delusional, and completely one-sided. The humour is the gap between how the walk is going in his head and how it's actually going.

How it was cut

Written as one running joke, not eight captions. The whole clip is a single escalating bit — confidence → bonding → rejection → denial → punchline. Each caption pays off the one before it, so people stay to the end to see how delusional Jonny gets.

The hook reframes what you're looking at. "You meet the BIGGEST dog ever" over a shot of a cow does the comedy instantly — the viewer gets the joke before Jonny does.

The last line is the payoff. "Made a new friend" lands big, then a small print "*he did not agree to this" undercuts it — a two-tier caption so the joke has a button.

Graded and cleaned. Shot on an overcast morning, so contrast and colour were lifted to make the greens pop, with a light denoise before sharpening. Punch-ins (1.08–1.18×) keep Jonny and the cow large enough to read on a phone.

Audio is quiet ambience by design. The source is a near-silent outdoor walk; it's kept low as background so the captions carry the whole clip. Add music at upload.
