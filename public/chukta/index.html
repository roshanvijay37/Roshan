What it does

  • Add each loan once — EMIs post themselves on their due dates
  • Total debt, what is due this month, and the month you will be debt-free
  • Prepayments, top-ups and statement corrections
  • Reports: how much went to interest, and CSV / backup downloads

Same account as the website, chukta.roshanvijay.com.

Installing from this page

  1. Tap the download button and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open Chukta and sign in, or create an account.

Chukta is also being tested on Google Play; testers who joined get updates there.
