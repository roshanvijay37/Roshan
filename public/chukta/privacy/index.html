Chukta

Privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026 · Android app com.roshanvijay.chukta and the website chukta.roshanvijay.com · Developer: Sumathi Enterprises

In short. Chukta keeps the loans, EMIs and payments you enter, under your own account, so it can show what you owe and post each EMI on its due date. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing. You can have everything deleted.

1. Who this policy is from

Chukta is published by Sumathi Enterprises, Belvai, Dakshina Kannada, Karnataka, India — the developer that publishes the app on Google Play. This policy covers the Chukta Android app and the Chukta website, which use the same account and the same data.

2. What Chukta collects

WhatWhy
Your email address and a passwordTo create your account and sign you in. The password is stored only as a secure hash by the sign-in service; nobody can read it.
Your name, as you type it at sign-upTo greet you in the app.
Loans you add: name, lender, amount outstanding, EMI, interest rate, due day, term, start date, original amount, and a noteTo show your debt, post each EMI automatically and work out when each loan finishes.
Bank accounts you name as the account an EMI is paid from (a name only — no account number, no balance)To show which account each EMI leaves from.
Entries: EMIs posted by the app, and prepayments, top-ups and corrections you record (amount, date, accounts, an optional note)To keep each loan's balance and your reports correct.
Scheduled payments you add (name, amount, due date, repeat, pay-from account)To deduct them on their due date and show what is due this month.

Chukta does not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, phone number, device identifiers or advertising ID, and it does not read your SMS or bank statements. It does not connect to your bank. The only Android permission it uses is Internet access.

3. What stays on your phone

  • Your sign-in session, in Android's encrypted storage, so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.
  • Your light / dark theme choice.
  • When you use Downloads in Reports, the CSV or backup file is written to the app's private cache and handed to the app you choose in the share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice.

Android's app backup is switched off for Chukta, so none of this is copied to a cloud backup.

4. Where your data is kept

Your account and everything you enter are stored in a database run by Supabase (supabase.com), in its Mumbai, India region (AWS ap-south-1). Supabase also sends the account emails (confirming your address and resetting your password). The website is served from a server in Mumbai (Amazon Lightsail); the Android app talks to the database directly.

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Every row is tied to your account and the database itself refuses to return one account's data to another (row-level security).

5. Sharing

Your data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone for advertising or marketing. It is processed only by the service providers named above, to run Chukta. It would be disclosed to an authority only where the law requires it.

6. How long it is kept

For as long as you have an account. An entry you remove in the app is hidden from every screen and total straight away, and is kept in the database (marked as removed) until your account is deleted. Loans can be archived. Deleting your account deletes all of it — see below.

7. Deleting your account and data

In the app: open the menu on your initials → Delete account. Or follow the steps on roshanvijay.com/chukta/delete-data. Your account and every loan, entry and scheduled payment in it are deleted within 7 days of the request.

8. Children

Chukta is a personal finance tool meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 18.

9. Financial information

Chukta is a record-keeping tool. It does not lend money, offer credit, move money or give financial advice. The figures it shows are worked out from what you enter.
