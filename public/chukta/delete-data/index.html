Delete your Chukta account

Chukta by Sumathi Enterprises · Android app com.roshanvijay.chukta · Updated 4 October 2026

How to ask

  1. From the email address you signed up with, write to roshanshetty010100@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Chukta account.
  2. Nothing else is needed. If the request comes from a different address, we reply to the account's address to confirm it is you before deleting anything.
  3. Your account and all of its data are deleted within 7 days, and you get a reply when it is done.
Email a deletion request

In the app the same page opens from the menu on your initials → Delete account.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your sign-in account: email address, password hash and nameDeleted
Every loan and bank account name you addedDeleted
Every entry — posted EMIs, prepayments, top-ups, corrections, including ones you had removedDeleted
Every scheduled paymentDeleted

Nothing is kept afterwards. Chukta has no other copy of your data: no analytics, no ads, no marketing lists. The database provider (Supabase) may hold its own short-lived operational backups, which age out on their own schedule.

Deleting some data without closing the account

  • An entry — on the loan's page, the ⋮ next to it → Delete. It disappears from every screen and total at once; it stays in the database, marked removed, until the account is deleted.
  • A scheduled payment — on the dashboard, its ⋮ → Delete. It is deleted outright.
  • A loan — on its page, Archive. It stops counting; to remove a loan entirely, email the address above with its name.

On your phone

Signing out removes the session the app keeps. Uninstalling Chukta removes everything it stored on the phone.
