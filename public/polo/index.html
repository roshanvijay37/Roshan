How it was cut

Cut from the 4K master, not the 1080p. A 9:16 crop keeps only 56% of a 16:9 frame's width — from 4K that is still 1215px, so the finished 1080-wide reel involves no upscaling anywhere.

Every shot got its own crop offset. A plain centre crop fails on this footage: the car sits left of centre in the rear shots, right of centre at the front-on reveal, and one framing lands on empty road.

Silent by design. The source is one continuous voiceover at a low, even level, so there is no per-shot sound to cut with — nine hard cuts through it would sound broken. Add a track at upload instead, where the platform's own library is licensed.
