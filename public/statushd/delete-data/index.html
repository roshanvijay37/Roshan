Delete your data

StatusHD — com.statushd

Last updated: 19 September 2026

There is no account to delete. StatusHD has no sign-up, no password and no profile. What can exist is a video you chose to upload, and the phone number you typed so it could be delivered. Both can be removed, and this page is how.

What is held, and where

WhatWhere it is
A video you uploaded with Send to server On a personal computer operated by Roshan Vijay in Mangalore, India
The phone number you typed for that upload The same computer — the prepared file is named after it
The video the app prepared on your phone, and its gallery entry Only on your own phone

Videos you never uploaded

If you only used Optimize video, nothing was ever sent anywhere and there is nothing here to delete. Removing the app, or deleting the videos from your gallery, removes all of it.

Videos you did upload — how to have them deleted

Send an e-mail asking for deletion. Include the phone number you typed and roughly when you sent it, because that is how the file is found.

roshanshetty010100@gmail.com

  • Requests are actioned by hand, normally within a few days.
  • Everything held for that number is deleted: the file you uploaded and the prepared copy made from it.
  • You will get a reply confirming it is done.
  • You do not have to explain why.

One thing this cannot reach. If the video was already delivered to WhatsApp, a copy is in that WhatsApp conversation and on Meta's servers. Deleting it there is done inside WhatsApp, by deleting the message — it is not something this request can do for you.

How long things are kept otherwise

Uploaded videos are not deleted automatically. They stay on that computer until they are removed by hand or you ask for them to go. Half-finished uploads, where the connection dropped and was never resumed, are swept after 24 hours.

Removing the app

Uninstalling removes everything StatusHD keeps on your phone. It has no effect on a video you already uploaded — use the e-mail above for that.
