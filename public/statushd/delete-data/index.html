Deleting your data

StatusHD — com.statushd

Last updated: 21 September 2026

In normal use there is nothing to delete.

A video sent to the StatusHD service is deleted from that computer as soon as the optimised copy has been delivered back to you. One that could not be delivered is deleted automatically after 48 hours. Neither needs a request.

What exists, and where

WhatWhere it isHow long
The video you sent, and the optimised copy made from it The service computer Deleted on delivery; after 48 hours if delivery failed
The WhatsApp message identifier of each video handled The service computer Kept, so the same video is never answered twice. It is an identifier, not the video, and cannot be turned back into one
The name your conversation appears under, and a count of videos returned to it The service computer Only if free-use limits are switched on. Kept until you ask for it to go
The copy the app staged before handing it to WhatsApp Only on your own phone, in the app's private cache Replaced the next time you send something; gone when you uninstall
The messages themselves Your WhatsApp conversation, and Meta's servers Until you delete them in WhatsApp

Asking for deletion

Send an e-mail from, or naming, the WhatsApp number you sent from — that is how the conversation is found. There is no account and no other identifier to quote.

roshanshetty010100@gmail.com

  • Requests are actioned by hand, normally within a few days.
  • Everything held for that conversation is deleted: any file still present, the record that the videos were handled, and any count kept against it.
  • You will get a reply confirming it is done.
  • You do not have to explain why.
One thing this cannot reach.

The video and the reply are both messages in your WhatsApp conversation, so copies exist there and on Meta's servers. Deleting those is done inside WhatsApp, by deleting the messages. It is not something this request can do for you.

Removing the app

Uninstalling removes everything StatusHD keeps on your phone, which is only the staged copy of the last video you sent. It has no effect on anything already sent through WhatsApp — use the e-mail above, and WhatsApp itself, for that.
