Privacy Policy

StatusHD — com.statushd

Last updated: 19 September 2026

StatusHD can upload your video, and you decide when. Optimising a video on your phone sends nothing. Tapping Send to server uploads that video, and the phone number you type, to a computer operated by Roshan Vijay in Mangalore, India. Nothing is uploaded unless you tap that button.

What is collected, and only when you tap Send to server

WhatWhy
The video file you choseIt is prepared for WhatsApp, which cannot be done without it
The phone number you typeIt decides which WhatsApp conversation the finished video is delivered to
The original file name and sizeSent with the upload so it can be resumed if the connection drops

There is no account, no sign-in, no password and no e-mail address. No analytics or crash-reporting service is used. No advertising identifier is read. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising, and nothing is used to build a profile of you.

Optimising on the phone sends nothing

The Optimize video route reads, processes and writes the video entirely on your own device. No network connection is made. The app also reads the videos already on your phone so you can pick one; that list never leaves the device.

What happens to a video you do upload

  • It travels over an encrypted HTTPS connection, through Cloudflare, which carries the traffic to the computer described above.
  • It is written to that computer's disk as an ordinary file, named after the phone number you gave.
  • It is re-encoded so WhatsApp will accept it without ruining the quality.
  • It is placed into WhatsApp and sent to the number you gave.

Be aware of two things. That computer is a personal machine, not a data centre, and the person who operates it can open and watch any file on it. And uploaded videos are not deleted automatically — they remain on that disk until they are removed by hand. If either of those is not acceptable to you, use the on-device route, which uploads nothing.

Who else sees it

  • Cloudflare carries the upload in transit.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) receives the finished video, because delivering it to you through WhatsApp is the point. Their handling is governed by their own policy.

Nobody else. There is no other third party, no data broker and no advertising network.
