Privacy Policy

StatusHD — com.statushd

Last updated: 21 September 2026

The app cannot send anything anywhere.

StatusHD does not request the INTERNET permission. That is not a policy it promises to follow — it is something the app is incapable of doing. It has no way to open a network connection, upload a file, or contact any server, including ours.

What it does is hand your chosen video to WhatsApp, and WhatsApp sends it, under its own permissions, when you choose to send it.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no password, no e-mail address and no phone number. No analytics or crash-reporting service is used. No advertising identifier is read. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to build a profile of you.

What the app does on your phone

  • Lists the videos already on your phone so you can pick one. That list never leaves the device.
  • Copies the video you pick into the app's own private cache, so it can be handed over under a name the service recognises. That copy is deleted the next time you send something, and when you uninstall the app.
  • Opens WhatsApp with that copy attached, as a document. Sending it is your action, in WhatsApp, and you choose who to send it to.

What happens after you send it

If you send it to the StatusHD service number, it arrives as a WhatsApp message on a personal computer operated by Roshan Vijay in Mangalore, India, which is signed in to WhatsApp like any other device. There, the video is:

  • downloaded from the conversation;
  • re-encoded so WhatsApp will accept it without ruining the quality;
  • sent back into the same conversation as a video;
  • deleted from that computer once it has been delivered. A prepared copy that could not be delivered is deleted automatically after 48 hours.
Two things to be aware of.

That computer is a personal machine, not a data centre, and the person who operates it can open and watch any file on it while it is there.

And once a video has been sent through WhatsApp, a copy exists in that WhatsApp conversation and on Meta's servers. Removing it there is done inside WhatsApp, by deleting the message. Nothing on this page can do that for you.

Who else sees it

  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries the video in both directions, because delivering it through WhatsApp is the entire point. Their handling is governed by their own policy.

Nobody else. There is no other third party, no data broker and no advertising network. Earlier versions of this page mentioned Cloudflare; that route no longer exists and no traffic passes through it.

There is no INTERNET permission, and no notification, location, contacts, camera or microphone permission. Earlier versions of this page listed INTERNET, POST_NOTIFICATIONS and FOREGROUND_SERVICE; none of them is declared any more.

Children

StatusHD is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Removing your video

Videos are deleted from that computer automatically once delivered, so in normal use there is nothing left to remove. If you want anything held for your conversation deleted anyway — including the record that it was handled — ask at the address below. See data deletion.
