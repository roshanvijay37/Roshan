Privacy Policy

StatusHD — com.statushd.app

Last updated: 17 September 2026

StatusHD collects no data at all. It has no server, no account and no analytics. Your videos are read, processed and written entirely on your own device and are never transmitted anywhere.

This is verifiable, not just a promise

StatusHD does not request the Android INTERNET permission. Without it, Android itself prevents the app from opening any network connection — so it could not upload your videos, or anything else, even if it were asked to. You can confirm this yourself: the permission list on the Google Play listing shows no network access.

What the app does with your videos

  • Reads the video you pick, using your phone's own media APIs.
  • Analyses it and re-encodes it with your phone's hardware video encoder.
  • Writes the result to your device, in Movies/StatusHD, only when you tap Save.
  • Hands the file to another app — WhatsApp, Instagram or anything else — only when you tap Share, using Android's standard share sheet. What that app then does with it is governed by its own privacy policy, not this one.

What is stored on your device

  • Optimised videos you chose to save, in your normal gallery storage.
  • A small list of those videos (file name, date, size, resolution) kept in the app's private storage so the "My Optimized Videos" screen can show them. Deleting the app removes it.
  • Temporary working files in the app's cache during processing, cleared automatically.

None of this leaves the device. None of it is readable by other apps.

Permissions, and why each one exists

  • Notifications — to show encoding progress if you leave the app. Optional.
  • Foreground service — so encoding is not killed when the app is backgrounded.
  • Read video — optional, and requested only if you open the in-app video library that lists your videos sorted by size. Decline it and the app still works normally through the system picker or file browser.
  • Storage write — on Android 9 and older only, to save into your gallery. Android 10 and newer do not need it.

Children

StatusHD is a general-purpose video utility. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

There are no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no crash-reporting SDKs in StatusHD. No third party receives any information from this app.
