How it works

Posting a video to Status compresses it hard. StatusHD gets around that by never letting WhatsApp's media path touch it:

  1. Once, the first time: save StatusHD as a contact. The app opens your Contacts with the name and number filled in; you press Save.
  2. Pick a video in the app — it lists yours biggest first, since those are the ones that suffer most.
  3. Tap Send to WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens the StatusHD chat with your video attached as a document, so it is transferred byte for byte instead of being re-compressed. You press send.
  4. An HD version comes back into the same chat, as a video.
  5. Forward that message to My Status. Forward it rather than saving it and posting it again from your gallery, which would compress it once more.

The app sends nothing by itself

StatusHD does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot open a network connection at all. It does not ask for your contacts either — your phone's own Contacts app saves the number. It hands your video to WhatsApp, and you send it. See the privacy policy.

Installing

  • Open it from your notification shade or Downloads.
  • Android will ask to allow installs from your browser — allow it once.
  • If you already have StatusHD from this page, this installs straight over it.
  • If you installed it from Google Play, uninstall that copy first. Play signs apps with its own key, so this one will refuse to install over it.
