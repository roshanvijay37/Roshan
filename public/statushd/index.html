What's new in 1.5.0

This release is about how the video gets into WhatsApp. A clip that looked sharp in the preview could still come out soft once posted — not because of the encode, but because the old Share to WhatsApp button took the worse of the two available routes.

  1. Save & open WhatsApp is now the main button. The file goes to Movies/StatusHD and WhatsApp opens with nothing attached, so you pick the video from WhatsApp's own gallery — the screen where its quality controls actually live.
  2. Sharing now saves to the gallery first. A file living only in the app's cache could not be picked from inside WhatsApp at all.
  3. A four-step guide on the result screen, including the send-to-yourself-then-forward method for Status. That one is widely reported to work but is not documented by WhatsApp, and the app says so rather than pretending otherwise.
  4. Direct sharing is still there, one row further down.

Why it matters: WhatsApp's HD toggle is a chat feature. Status has no HD control of its own, so a Status post is re-encoded at standard quality no matter what it was handed.
