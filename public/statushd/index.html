What's new in 1.7.0

Targets 720p now — because that is what your phone actually sends.

Measured across three round trips on a Galaxy S24 Ultra: WhatsApp's Android app will not send video above 1280×720, HD enabled or not. A 144 MB HEVC original, a 71 MB H.264 re-encode and a 98 MB 720p file all came back 720×1280 at ~3.2 Mbps — within 2.6 KB of each other.

  1. Keeping 1080p preserved a resolution the phone always discarded, and handed WhatsApp's own scaler the job of removing it. StatusHD does that reduction now, with a real bicubic filter instead of the GPU's bilinear sampler — worth +3.65 VMAF end to end.
  2. The filter is the feature. Dropping to 720p using the default bilinear sampler scores 55.85 against 54.96 for doing nothing — under a point. A proper bicubic scores 61.10.
  3. Instagram is unchanged and still targets 1080p. It transcodes on its servers, and nothing about it has been measured.

Not verified on hardware. The new downscale shader has never run on a GPU. If it is wrong you will see obvious corruption rather than slight softness — check one video first.
