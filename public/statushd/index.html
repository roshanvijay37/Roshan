How it works

  1. Say hello on WhatsApp

    Tap the green button and message us. We'll add your number — start with a free 7-day trial.

    You'll see ⏳ on your file while we work on it. Your HD video comes back as a reply to it, and the ⏳ turns into ✅.

    Tap the video we sent → Forward → My status. That's it.
