What this is. A working prototype made from the single photo you sent — the product was cut out of its white studio background programmatically, a contact shadow was generated from its own silhouette, and a specular sheen is masked to that same silhouette so the light reacts as it turns. Everything moves on one scroll timeline.

What it is not. There is no 3D model here, so the sandal cannot be turned past roughly ±15° or viewed from the sole. The construction layers are tinted silhouettes of the same outline, not real geometry, and the specs are placeholder copy. For a true 3D build the next step is a modelled or photogrammetry-scanned .glb.
