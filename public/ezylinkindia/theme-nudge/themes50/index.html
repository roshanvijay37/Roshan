50 colour themes, grouped by taste

From 8 to 50: seven groups for different kinds of people, from clean whites and soft pastels to dark modes, bold colours, India's festivals, nature, and extra-readable options. Every theme below is shown on the real home screen.

Every one is easy to read. Each theme is made from 2–3 base colours, and the rest is worked out automatically. A readability check (the standard accessibility contrast test) passes on all 50: header text, button text, links and filled buttons.

Your current 8 stay exactly as they are. The other 42 are marked NEW. The header uses the palette icon you picked (Way B).

The new picker

Fifty cards need a different picker: chips to jump to a group, a short "who it's for" line per group, smaller cards three to a row, and a Surprise me button for people who can't decide.
