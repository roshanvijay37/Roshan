ezylinkindia — which colours?

These are mock screens of the new ezylinkindia app in seven other colour schemes, next to today’s Midnight & Saffron. Nothing in the app has changed. The layout, text and buttons are identical in every option; only the colours are different.

Want the app without the dark header? See the colourful and fully light options (H to P).

How to use this page
  1. Scroll the strip below to compare the home screen in every colour.
  2. Tap Open on any option to see home, categories, search results, a business page and the desktop website in that colour.
  3. Tap ⬇ Download to save one picture and share it on WhatsApp.
  4. Reply with the letter you like best.
