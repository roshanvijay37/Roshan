Delete your data

Everything Ezy Photo Uploader holds about you sits either on your own phone or in your own Google Drive. You can remove all of it yourself, without asking anyone.

App Ezy Photo Uploader Developer Roshan Vijay Package com.roshanvijay.ezyphotouploader
There is no account to delete. The app has no server and no sign-up — it never creates an account for you, and the developer holds no copy of your photographs or your details. That is why deletion is something you carry out directly rather than request.

Steps to delete your data

  1. Delete an individual event Open the app, choose the event, and delete it. That removes the photographs held on the phone for that event, along with its queue entries and log.
  2. Remove everything on the phone Uninstall Ezy Photo Uploader. Android deletes the private storage of the app with it — every photograph still on the device, the queue database, your settings and the diagnostic log. Nothing is left behind.
  3. Delete the photographs already in your Drive Open Google Drive, find the folder Ezy Photo Uploader, and delete it or any event folder inside it. Then empty the Drive bin to remove them permanently.
  4. Withdraw the app's access to your Drive Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select Ezy Photo Uploader, and remove access. The app can then no longer read or write anything in your Drive.
  5. Turn off any link you shared If you generated a QR code for an event, open that event in the app and switch sharing off — or change the folder back to private in Google Drive. Anyone holding the old link loses access immediately.

No retention period applies to anything held by the developer, because the developer holds nothing. There is no server, no database and no backup outside your own devices and your own Drive.
