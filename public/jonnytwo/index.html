How it was cut

Cut from a 4K 60fps take, not a phone export. The source is 2160×3840 — so every punch-in (up to 1.35×) crops into real 4K pixels and still lands on a sharp 1080-wide frame. No upscaling anywhere.

Reordered into an escalation. The single 49-second take is one long play-and-train session; the shots were picked and re-sequenced into a rising list — sit → up → stand → catch — with the most eye-catching move (standing on hind legs) pulled to 0:00 as a cold-open hook.

The climax is timed to the frame. The "catch" is a jump-and-grab, not a clean hold, so the shot is trimmed to end on the grab and the word GOT IT is placed on the exact frame his mouth closes on the mat — a beat later and it would read as a miss.

Graded to rescue the backlight. The first half is shot straight into a bright window, so Jonny came out dark. Shadows and midtones are lifted (gamma + brightness), colour and contrast pushed, and a light denoise applied before sharpening so the lift doesn't reveal grain.

Captions sit in the top third, clear of both the dog and the platform's button rail, in Poppins ExtraBold with a gold accent on the key word.

No voiceover, on purpose. The source audio is family chatter in the room (a transcriber couldn't resolve it above ~37% confidence). It's kept as quiet ambience and normalised to −14.2 LUFS; the captions carry the story.
